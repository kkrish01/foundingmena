The Women Behind Some of MENA’s Biggest Companies
Trendyol is one of Turkey’s biggest technology companies. Insider sells its software to more than 1,500 brands across 28 countries. Huda Beauty became a global cosmetics business, while Hepsiburada became the first Turkish company to list on Nasdaq. They were built in different cities, across different industries, and at very different moments in the region’s startup history. But each had a woman on the founding team. Across MENA, women have helped build some of the region’s largest companies in e-commerce, enterprise software, beauty, logistics, fintech and investing. Their stories also show how much broader the region’s entrepreneurial landscape has become.
